Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines how your personal information and privacy data are handled in the mobile application "LiteraryFragments" (hereinafter referred to as the "App").

1. Information Collection and Use

The App collects the following information solely for the purposes described below:

  • Location Data (GPS): When using the "Sense the Moment" feature, the App temporarily requests your location to fetch the current weather and time of day. This is used strictly to recommend a quote that matches your current atmosphere. Your location data is NEVER stored on our servers, tracked, or shared with third parties.
  • In-App History: Your stocked (favorite) quotes and reading history are saved locally on your device. The developer does not collect, access, or store this data.

2. Third-Party Services

The App does not sell or share your personal information with third parties. However, to provide its core features, the App communicates with the following external APIs without sending any personally identifiable information (PII):

  • Google Gemini API: The App sends the text of the selected quote to generate AI explanations and translations.
  • Open-Meteo API: The App sends temporary coordinate data to retrieve weather conditions.

3. In-App Purchases

The App uses Apple Inc.'s secure payment system (StoreKit) for ticket purchases. All credit card and payment information is processed and managed entirely by Apple Inc. The developer does not collect or have access to your payment details.

4. Disclaimer

The developer is not responsible for the accuracy of the translations or AI-generated explanations provided by the App. Furthermore, the developer shall not be held liable for any damages or troubles arising from the use of the App.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be effective immediately upon posting on this page.
